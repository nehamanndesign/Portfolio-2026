June 2025 – Present Senior Product Designer Word, Excel, PowerPoint · Microsoft

Word, Excel, and PowerPoint are being fundamentally rethought. AI is changing what these tools can do — not just adding features, but reshaping how people actually work inside them. I lead design across three bets: letting people hold parallel work without losing context, teaching the suite to recognize what's actually time-sensitive, and building recommendations that learn from how each person works. The throughline is trust — getting the suite to step in exactly when it's useful, and stay out of the way everywhere else.

Nov 2020 – June 2024 Designer II Microsoft Stream

At Stream, I designed the AI layer that made long video usable. Auto-chaptering gave hours of footage a structure it didn't ship with. Points of Interest surfaced the moments worth returning to. Copilot for video tied it together — letting people search, summarize, and navigate video the way they'd navigate a document. Across all three, the goal was the same: nobody should have to scrub through an hour of footage to find the thirty seconds that matter.

Copilot for video

Bringing AI into video the way people actually watch it — summaries, search, and navigation built around moments, not timestamps.
